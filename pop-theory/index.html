Manifesto · five laws

The laws of pop theory.

Bubble tea got loud and a bit careless. We think it deserves craft and a sense of humour, in that order.

  1. Law I

    Texture is a flavour.

    Chew counts. Nobody remembers a drink they didn’t have to bite.

  2. Law II

    Sweetness is a dial, not a default.

    Zero to full. You decide; we calibrate.

  3. Law III

    Never stir what should swirl.

    The syrup finds its own way down. Give it a minute.

  4. Law IV

    The straw goes in last.

    It isn’t a garnish. It’s a full stop.

  5. Law V

    Be serious about play.

    Premium doesn’t have to whisper.

End of theory

That’s the theory.

The rest is practice. Go and find a straw.
