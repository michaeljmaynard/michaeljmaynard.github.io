A layout for how you plan

The first choice is how much of your time you want to see at once. An open spread makes the difference between a day on its own page and a week at a glance visible. The diary sketch beside the questions carries that choice forward.

Size you can picture

Desk, Mid-Size and Pocket sit against the same sheet of A4, giving each size a familiar point of reference. The historical size guide adds model-by-model measurements for anyone who wants a more precise comparison.

TrySwitch from Desk to Pocket, then compare a model in the size guide.

03 of 05

Colour in context

Three colour families keep the choice manageable, with original photography showing the range within each. Individual swatches tint the diary sketch so you can explore shades while keeping your broader colour preference.

TryChoose The Bright Side, then tap a swatch to see it on the diary sketch.

04 of 05

A closer look at leather

Close-up photographs let the grain and finish do the explaining. Alongside each photograph, the diary sketch shows how the texture might look at your chosen size and in an approximate colour.

TrySwitch between Mara, Panama and Ludlow and look at how the grain changes.
