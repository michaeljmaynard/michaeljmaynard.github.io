      The shirt in detail

      The cloth

      A twill that comes out of the wash ready.

      Twill is woven on a diagonal, which gives a white shirt a faint sheen and a softer drape than poplin. The non-iron finish means it hangs dry and is ready to wear.

      The collar

      The full cutaway, the Windsor way.

      The points sweep wide so a full knot sits centred, with nothing crowding it. Hawes & Curtis files this collar under the Windsor name, after its most famous early customer.

      1. 1913Ralph Hawes and Freddie Curtis open in Piccadilly Arcade, at the corner of Jermyn Street.
      2. 1922First Royal Warrant, from the then Prince of Wales, later Duke of Windsor.
