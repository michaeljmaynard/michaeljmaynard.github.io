01 of 05

Choosing the shirt

Colour, fit, collar and sleeve sit together in one form. Until both sizes are chosen, the line above Add to bag asks for them; once they are, it names the exact shirt. The multibuy sits beside the price, in one colour kept for offers.

TryPick a collar and a sleeve, then read the line above Add to bag.

02 of 05

Four shirts, one week

The four-shirt offer becomes a working week. Each day takes a shirt in your size and, if you like, a tie. Two meters show how close the week is to each offer, and the whole week goes to the bag in one step.

TryAdd a shirt for Tuesday.

03 of 05

Shop the look

The outfit in the main photo is shoppable from the photo itself. Shop the look opens a side panel listing the shirt, tie and trousers in the shot. Each has its own Include tick, and the shirt and trouser sizes are chosen there. Whatever is ticked goes to the bag together, so one shirt can become a full outfit without leaving the page.

TryPress Shop the look on the main product image.

04 of 05

Sizing with confidence

Fit is drawn rather than described: classic, slim and extra slim side by side. The measurement table highlights the collar you have chosen, in inches or centimetres. Find my size turns a neck measurement into a collar size, and says plainly that it is guidance.

TrySwitch the table to centimetres, or use Find my size beside the size buttons.

The story, after the decision

Below the buying decisions, the page slows down: what a twill weave does, why this collar is cut wide, and two dates from the house’s history. It is there for anyone who wants it, without standing between the shirt and the bag.
